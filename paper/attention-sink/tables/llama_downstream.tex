\caption{\textbf{Downstream HotpotQA on fully-softmax LLaMA, deployed at a constant sliding window of $W$ tokens (the model attends only the most recent $W$ tokens, never the full context).} All models start from the pretrained LLaMA-3.2 \emph{base} checkpoint (not instruction-tuned), are continued-pretrained on the same WikiText with ordinary next-token prediction under each scheme's attention regime (\emph{only} the mask/format differs, never the data or the task), and are evaluated \emph{zero-shot} on HotpotQA.
